Questions

Things people ask before they book.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a short call we send a fixed quote with the deliverables listed. A typical marketing site lands in the middle of our range, and sprints cost less than full projects.

Can you work with our existing brand?

Yes. We start by auditing your current identity and keep what works. If parts of it do not hold up on a screen, we tell you which ones and why.

Who owns the files when we finish?

You do. We hand over the design files, the code and the documentation, and walk your team through them.

Do you build with WordPress or Shopify only?

No. Those are two platforms we know well, and we also build custom sites and web apps. We recommend whichever tool your team can actually run after launch.

What do you need from us to start?

A short brief, access to any current site, and one person who can make decisions. We handle the rest of the setup in the first week.
