About the studio

Design and code, one conversation.

DRAV is a studio of designers who write code and developers who care about type. We keep the team small so the person who draws the button is the person who ships it.

Prototype in code

We test ideas in the browser early, so layout and motion are decided with real constraints.

Tokens, not guesses

Every color, size and space is named once and reused, so the site stays consistent as it grows.

Hand over everything

You get the files, the documentation and a walkthrough. No lock-in.

Process

Four stages, usually eight weeks.

Timings are for a typical marketing site. Larger builds add weeks to the middle stage and nowhere else.

  1. 1 Week 1

    Discover

    We learn your goals, audience and constraints, and look hard at what exists today.

    BriefAudit
  2. 2 Weeks 1–2

    Define

    Scope, sitemap and content structure get agreed before any pixels are pushed.

    SitemapScope
  3. 3 Weeks 2–6

    Design & Build

    Screens and code move together. You review working pages, not static pictures.

    DesignStaging site
  4. 4 Weeks 6–8

    Refine & Launch

    We test on real devices, fix the small things, launch, and stay on for the first month.

    QALaunchHandover

Case study

Sable Atlas: from spreadsheet to dashboard.

A logistics team tracked 40 routes across five tools. We rebuilt it as one fast dashboard on a shared component library.

Challenge
Dispatchers needed route status in seconds, and the old tools took minutes to load.
Approach
We prototyped the dashboard in code, tested it with dispatchers, then built tokens and components.
Result
One screen, one source of truth, and a library the client's team now extends on its own.
